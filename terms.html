1. What CV Adapter is

CV Adapter is a browser extension that reads a job ad you capture from LinkedIn, compares it with a CV you keep in your own Google Docs, and proposes an adapted copy. It is a writing aid: it drafts text, and you decide what is kept. The extension is provided free of charge, and its source code is published on GitHub.
